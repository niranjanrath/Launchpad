Launchpad

Version 1.0.0 · Personal work-links launcher

Launchpad is a private start page for the tools you open every day. It runs entirely in your browser — there's no account, no server, and no data ever leaves your device. Everything you see is generated from a simple structure of links, categories and preferences stored in your browser's local storage.
